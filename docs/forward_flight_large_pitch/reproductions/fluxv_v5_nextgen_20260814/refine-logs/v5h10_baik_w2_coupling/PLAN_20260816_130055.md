## Decision

The three-layer run is eligible only after the global release-row owner and
the current v5h9 integrity gate pass. A three-layer pass authorizes a longer
raw W2 run, not an accuracy claim. Any support/lifecycle mismatch returns
`remesh_required` and stops without silently welding or tuning.

## Recorded pre-execution STOP and diagnosis route

A fresh no-GT replay of the first three active rows reached native Ptera loads
but stopped during the third row's rVPM transport.  With the frozen physical
birth radius and spacing, the minimum particle radius evolved as follows:

- source 4 / Ptera 3: `0.00152 -> 0.001483081575 m`;
- source 5 / Ptera 4: `0.001483081575 -> 0.001072830623 m`;
- source 6 / Ptera 5: stage 1 `0.000953186244 m`, then stage 2
  `-0.000561786112 m` (strict STOP).

The failing state had 3432 particles and `max(abs(J))` of order
`1.3e2 1/s`.  No candidate artifact was published and no candidate GT score
was computed.  A diagnostic dyadic-substep replay also showed that positivity
alone is insufficient: 1 substep failed, 2 passed, 4 failed, and 8/16 passed
but the 8-to-16 gamma relative L2 difference was about `2.33`.

Before any retry is eligible, the run contract therefore requires:

1. a no-GT `32/64` (and, if needed, `128`) transport-substep convergence
   study from the exact same third-row state;
2. a preregistered stability rule based on positivity and adjacent-resolution
   state/field convergence, never clipping sigma or changing the frozen core;
3. a real-scale three-row regression using the canonical W2 source and row
   committer, with every stage finite and sigma strictly positive;
4. persistent per-stage hashes and independent panel-to-total load checks in
   the 12-file convergence/raw bundle; and
5. an atomic `NOREPLACE` publication path and a fresh integrity audit.

Until all five close, dependency hashes remain deliberately unbound and the
formal raw run, GT access, filtering, and scoring remain blocked.

The retry matrix and thresholds are now frozen in `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` before
the complete-trajectory results are available: full three-layer `N=32,64,128`
runs are mandatory, `N=64` is the preselected candidate, and `N=128` is only
its verification reference.  The candidate may proceed only if the adjacent
state, tracer, probe, invariant, stability, load, and error-reduction gates all
pass.  This is a no-GT post-failure amendment; it does not rewrite the original
single-step failure or authorize outcome-dependent threshold changes.
